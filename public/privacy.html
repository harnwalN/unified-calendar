Privacy Policy

Unified Calendar · Last updated October 26, 2026

The short version

  • Unified Calendar only reads your calendars so it can show them to you.
  • Everything is stored in your own browser. There is no app database and no user accounts.
  • If you turn on Sync, your tasks and time logs are also kept in a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive — the app can’t see any of your other files.
  • We never sell, share, or analyze your data, and there are no ads or analytics.
  • You can disconnect and delete everything at any time.

What Unified Calendar is

Unified Calendar is a free, open-source web app that shows events from your Google Calendar accounts, your Canvas (learning management system) calendar feed, and other calendar subscription (.ics) links together in one view. It runs entirely in your web browser.

Google user data we access

When you choose to connect a Google account, the app asks Google for these permissions:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.readonly – read-only access to your calendar list and events.
  • openid, email, profile – your name, email address and profile picture, used only to label the connected account in the app.

Only if you turn on Sync in Settings, the app also asks for:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata – access to the app’s own hidden “application data” folder in your Google Drive. The app stores a single file there (unified-calendar.json) with your classes, task plans, notes, personal to-dos, time logs and imported PrairieLearn deadlines, so they can be shared between your own devices. This permission does not give access to any of your other Drive files, and the file is not visible in your Drive’s file list.

Apart from that one app-data file, the app cannot create, change, or delete anything in your Google account. Calendar data is used only to display your events and deadlines to you, inside the app, on your device.

How your data is handled and stored

  • In your browser: your settings, connected account details (name, email, picture), sign-in tokens, calendar feed links, checklist progress and a cache of your events are saved in your browser’s local storage and IndexedDB on your device. They never leave your device except as described below.
  • Directly from Google: your browser requests calendar data straight from Google’s APIs. Google user data is never sent to or stored on any server other than Google’s.
  • Sign-in helper: a small server (a Cloudflare Worker) is used only to exchange the Google sign-in code for tokens and to refresh access tokens, because that step requires a secret that can’t live in a web page. Tokens pass through it in transit and are returned to your browser; the helper does not store, log or inspect them, and it never receives your calendar data.
  • Canvas and other .ics feeds: your feed links are stored in your browser. Because most calendar servers don’t allow direct browser access, feed downloads may be relayed through the same helper, which passes the calendar file back to your browser without storing it.

Gradescope and PrairieLearn

  • Gradescope: the app recognizes Gradescope deadlines in your Google Calendar (for example ones added by the third-party “Gradescope to Cal” extension). It never contacts Gradescope itself.
  • PrairieLearn: the optional “Send to Calendar” bookmarklet runs only when you click it on a PrairieLearn page. It reads the course name and assessment deadlines shown on that page and passes them to this app inside the link it opens (after the #, which is never sent to any server).

What we don’t do

  • No analytics, tracking, cookies for advertising, or ads.
  • We don’t sell, rent, or share your data with anyone.
  • We don’t use your data to train AI or machine-learning models.
  • No humans read your data.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Unified Calendar’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Specifically, Google user data is used only to provide the user-facing features of displaying your own calendar events to you and syncing your own task data between your devices; it is not transferred to third parties, not used for advertising, not used to determine credit-worthiness or for lending, and not read by humans.

Revoking access and deleting your data

  • Remove one account: open Settings → Google accounts and click the remove button. This revokes the app’s token with Google and deletes that account’s data from your browser.
  • Stop syncing: open Settings → Sync and choose Off. To delete the synced file from your Drive, open Google Drive settings → Manage apps → Unified Calendar → Delete hidden app data.
  • Delete everything: open Settings → Privacy & data → Reset app. This signs out every account and erases all of the app’s data from your browser. Clearing your browser’s site data does the same.
  • Revoke from Google: visit myaccount.google.com/permissions and remove Unified Calendar at any time.

Since we don’t keep any copy of your data on our side (the optional sync file lives in your own Drive), there is nothing else to delete.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and doesn’t knowingly collect any data from them.
